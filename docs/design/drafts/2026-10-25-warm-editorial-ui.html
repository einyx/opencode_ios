Rules
· Serif (ui-serif / New York) for answers, screen titles, hero text
· Mono for every technical string: paths, tools, tokens, statuses
· Terracotta = brand actions (send, mic, active tab). Lake blue = information only (links, selection, focus)
· Liquid Glass: the composer, tab bar and photo button are translucent layers — blur(24) saturate(1.7), warm-white 62%, hairline white 60%. Content scrolls under them. Glass floats; parchment grounds · Hairlines instead of shadows; shadows only on the floating composer
· Dark mode: ink #262624, card #30302E, text #F5F4EF, same accents
