Privacy Policy for OpenCodeX

Last updated: 2026-10-26

OpenCodeX is an iOS, iPadOS, watchOS and visionOS client for OpenCode, the open-source AI coding agent. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

The short version

OpenCodeX has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It talks to the OpenCode server you choose, and to nothing else. The developers of the app never receive your prompts, your replies, your code or your credentials.

What the app stores on your device

The app stores the configuration needed to reach the servers you add:

  • the server address, transport (direct or SSH tunnel) and SSH settings;
  • the username and a reference to the credential you provide;
  • your server password or pairing token, stored in the system keychain;
  • the selected model and agent per server, your draft messages, and the transcripts the server has sent you, so the app can show them again;
  • your language preference and other interface settings.

What the app sends where

Everything the app sends goes to the OpenCode server you added:

  • your messages, the images you attach, and the agent or model you pick;
  • the tool calls an agent makes and their results, which the app displays;
  • the name of the file or folder you open in the file browser.

If you use the AI Usage Dashboard integration, the app also asks that dashboard for your provider's quota. It points at the dashboard you configure yourself.

Camera and microphone

  • The camera is used only when you scan a pairing QR code, and only when you tap the scan button.
  • The microphone is used when you dictate a message, or when you use Ambient mode to talk to the assistant.

Voice transcription

Voice input has two paths, and they are not the same:

  • On-device (default). Speech recognition runs on the device using Apple's frameworks. The recording is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Cloud (opt-in). If you select a cloud speech strategy in Settings → Input & Voice, the recording is uploaded to the speech service you configure there and only the transcript is returned. That service is a separate provider you choose; its own privacy policy and terms apply to the audio. The app does not ship a speech service of its own, and the developers do not receive the audio.

Optional Health export

The app can ask an agent on your server to export Health data. When you approve that request, the app opens the separate Health Quantification app with a one-time callback address. That app performs the export to the server it is configured with. The health details themselves are not returned through the callback, and they are not sent to the OpenCodeX developers.

Local network

The app asks for permission to reach devices on your local network so it can connect to an OpenCode server running on your computer.

Background audio

Ambient mode can keep recording while the app is in the background so a conversation is not cut off. The recording stops when you end the conversation.

No data collection by the developers

The developers of OpenCodeX do not operate a server for the app, do not collect analytics, and do not receive anything you type. Your data stays between your device and the servers you configured.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third-party open source

The app links open-source packages under permissive licences. Their licences are listed in the app under Settings → Open source licences.

Changes

If this policy is updated, the updated version will be published at the same URL and the date above will change.

Contact
